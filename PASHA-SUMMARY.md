WHAT IT DOES

- Every message from all your Facebook pages, Instagram, TikTok, and email
  shows up in one inbox. You or whoever's working that day can answer from
  one place instead of five.
- You wake up and see: how much you sold yesterday, how many messages came
  in, how many are still unread, what's getting delivered today.
- Every customer who ever bought from you is in one list — their email,
  phone, what they bought, when. You can pull that list any time to blast
  a sale to everyone.
- Shows exactly where your ad money is going and what it's actually
  bringing back, per platform — Facebook, Instagram, Google, TikTok.
- Makes Facebook/Instagram/TikTok reels for you automatically. Take
  photos of the furniture, pick a promo ("no credit needed", "Memorial Day
  sale", whatever), it writes the caption in English and Spanish and
  builds the video.
- Finds new places to advertise that nobody's tried yet — with real phone
  numbers and contact people, not just ideas. Already found the military
  base as a real option.
- You control who on your team can see what — messages, inventory,
  customers, money. You or I turn it on or off per person.
- Whoever's on the floor with a customer can pull up inventory and start
  the sale right from their phone.
